2. Measurement — supine / semi-recumbent
Maximum echo-free distance between the visceral and parietal pleura (posterior/mid-axillary line) — the same measurement is used by both the Eibenberger and Balik formulas.
Also include a Remerand multiplane measurement, if available
L = apical-to-caudal paravertebral effusion length; A = cross-sectional area at the midpoint between those limits (read directly off the ultrasound machine's area-trace tool). This formula has the strongest reported correlation to drained/CT volume of any option here (r=0.84–0.90), but the patient-positioning requirement from the original validation couldn't be confirmed from available sources — use it as a supplementary data point, not a replacement for the position-matched formulas above.
